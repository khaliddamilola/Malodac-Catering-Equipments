Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the things customers ask most.

How do I place an order?

Call or WhatsApp us on 0802 305 3256, or send a message through the contact page. Tell us which items you want and we will confirm the details with you.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we offer fast delivery to your doorstep. Delivery time and cost depend on your location, so contact us with your address and we will confirm both.

Can I order in large quantities for an event?

Yes. Tell us the type of event and how many guests you are serving, and we will help you work out what to order.

I don't know which equipment I need. Can you help?

Of course. Message us with your event details or the kind of food you serve and we will recommend suitable equipment.
